02 · Group workshop

🧠 Present Simple vs. Present Continuous Workshop

Team up and complete the workshop together. Discuss every answer and be ready to explain your decisions.

🌐 Open the workshop

Use the external interactive workshop to review and practise both tenses.

Open Present Simple vs. Present Continuous Workshop ↗

🤝 How to work

  1. Work in a small group.
  2. Read each sentence carefully.
  3. Discuss which tense fits the meaning.
  4. Explain your choice using clues and context.
  5. Help each other—not just give answers.

🔎 Remember

Present Simple: usually, often, every day, never, facts, routines.

Present Continuous: now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, temporary situations.

Time expressions help, but meaning is the most important clue.

💬 Group challenge

After completing the workshop, choose three answers your group found challenging. Explain why the tense is correct and create one new example for each tense.

13 · Final Mission

🏪 Final Mission — The Online Marketplace

Explore, practise, and complete the challenges with your team.

🏆 Your team mission

Create a mini online store with three products. Then become buyers and sellers and interact with other teams.

Choose a store theme: 📱 Technology · 👕 Fashion · 🎮 Gaming · ⚽ Sports · 📚 Books & entertainment · 🛍️ Random finds

🏷️ Store setup

🛍️ Buyer role

  • Ask about the product.
  • Ask about the condition.
  • Ask about the price.
  • Ask at least two follow-up questions.
  • Negotiate and decide.

💰 Seller role

  • Describe the product.
  • Explain its condition.
  • Give the price.
  • Persuade the customer.
  • Answer questions and offer a deal.

💬 Model conversation

Buyer: Hi! I'm interested in your headphones.

Seller: Hi! They're in excellent condition.

Buyer: How much are they?

Seller: They're $40.

Buyer: Are they wireless?

Seller: Yes, they connect to your phone via Bluetooth.

Buyer: How long does the battery last?

Seller: About ten hours.

Buyer: Can you give me a discount?

Seller: I can sell them for $35.

Buyer: Great! I'll buy them!
